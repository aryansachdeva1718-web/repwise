# e_1rm Research

Factors considered for intra-class correlation coefficient (ICC) and/or coefficient of variation (CV):
1. Resistance Training Experience
2. Familarization sessions
3. Single-joint or multi-joint exercises
4. Upper and lower body strength assessment
5. Gender (Male/Female)
6. Age (young to middle-aged adults and among older adults)
---

Training variables :
1. Intensity
2. Number of sets
3. Rest interval between sets
4. Exercise Modality - type of equipment/tool used
---

-> When training with loads between 50% and 90% of one repetition maximum, 3-5 minutes' rest between sets allowed for greater repetitions over multiple sets.

-> When testing maximal strength, 1-minute rest intervals might be sufficient between repeated attempts; however, from a psychological and physiological standpoint, the inclusion of 3- to 5-minute rest intervals might be safer and more reliable.

-> Indirectly demonstrated the benefits of utilizing short rest intervals for gains in muscular endurance.

-> Higher fatigue is induced as sets approach failure with high training volumes and densities, especially for protocols using light loads
